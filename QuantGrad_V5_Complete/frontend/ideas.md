# QuantGrad Design Directions

## Three exploratory directions

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
|---|---|---:|
| Cobalt Signal Desk | A midnight market terminal where cobalt confidence bands direct the eye through model states and execution context. | 0.06 |
| Archive of Prices | A restrained editorial trading journal with parchment, ink, and evidence-led market annotations. | 0.03 |
| Vector Field | A light, geometric data laboratory built around clean contour maps and analytical whitespace. | 0.08 |

## Chosen approach: Cobalt Signal Desk

**Design Movement.** Dark institutional quant terminal, influenced by market microstructure dashboards and technical research tools rather than generic AI products.

**Core Principles.** First, a fixed left-to-right intelligence flow reads as market selection, price evidence, then decision. Second, panels operate as quiet instruments around one dominant active market view. Third, cobalt indicates product action and model conviction; green and red only mean favourable and adverse market semantics. Fourth, every decorative shape must derive from a chart, confidence band, model topology, or liquidity vector.

**Color Philosophy.** Near-black navy gives data and decisions room to breathe. Electric cobalt is QuantGrad's ownable command color: used only for live action, selected state, and high-confidence model context. Icy blue supplies passive context. Green and coral remain semantic price movement colors and never become general decoration.

**Layout Paradigm.** The terminal is a decision river: the watchlist and model identity form the source at left, price and feature evidence occupy the wider central channel, and the decision ledger is the outlet at right. The hero is an entry threshold above the terminal rather than a centered marketing section.

**Signature Elements.** A five-segment aperture mark; a three-track cobalt signal topology, echoing the trend/structure/entry ensemble; and dotted confidence horizons inside charts and badges.

**Interaction Philosophy.** Controls react quickly and precisely: a selected market moves the entire workspace; a run action changes only data state, never the page structure; unavailable API data becomes an explicit preview state instead of an empty screen.

**Animation.** The terminal rises once with a 14px, 600ms decelerated entrance. Selected controls fade to cobalt in under 160ms. The topology lines may drift subtly only when motion is permitted; no looping decorative pulse competes with market values.

**Typography System.** Space Grotesk delivers compact high-authority decisions and section claims; Manrope keeps explanatory prose readable; DM Mono identifies telemetry, labels, prices, and system timestamps. A model decision is always visibly larger than its supporting conditions.

**Brand Essence.** QuantGrad is the model-led market intelligence desk for traders who need signal context before execution. **Precise, disciplined, alert.**

**Brand Voice.** Headlines are concise, analytical, and consequential; calls to action name the operation. Examples: “Decode the market before the noise does.” and “Refresh model read.”

**Wordmark & Logo.** The logo is a five-segment aperture/Q mark with a cobalt core and a narrow technical wordmark; it reappears as a small model-state glyph and in signal topology nodes.

**Signature Brand Color.** QuantGrad Cobalt — `#2F6BFF`.

## Style Decisions

- QuantGrad uses chart-derived signal motifs, confidence bands, and model-state glyphs; generic AI blobs are excluded.
- The display/telemetry pairing is Space Grotesk, Manrope, and DM Mono; the highest-value model decision must visibly dominate secondary information.
- Electric cobalt is the product/action color. Green and coral are reserved strictly for market semantics.
